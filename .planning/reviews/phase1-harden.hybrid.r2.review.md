## Security & data-loss

- [ ] **medium — `quarantineDrift()` propagates every fs error, turning a single unreadable placement into a total sync failure** — `src/lib/placer.ts:112` *(merges 3 reviewer reports across Security & data-loss / Reuse, simplify, perf / Terum invariants)*
  Only `lstat` is guarded (placer.ts:111); the following `snapshotSkillDirectory(path)` walks with `readdir`/`readFile`, so EACCES, ENOTDIR, EMFILE, or an ENOENT from a dangling symlink escapes.   **Fix:** keep the fail-closed contract inside `remove()` (where falling through to `rm` was the original data-loss bug), but restore per-placement tolerance: wrap sync.ts:111 in a try/catch that emits `notice(\`Blocked ${path}: could not read the placement (…); leaving it untouched.\`)` and `continue`s. install.ts:102 is the sibling call site and may keep throwing (it fails only that one install).
